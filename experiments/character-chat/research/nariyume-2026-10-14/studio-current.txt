An opening note ·
9 September 2026
Start with a conversation
A café shift. An unfinished sketch. A game that Yume insists she should have won. NariYume starts with the small details that give a character a life beyond a single reply.
Type a message, hear an answer, and watch her react. The aim is a conversation with texture: different opinions, shared moments and details that can carry into the next visit.
Meet Nari
Nari works at a café and dreams of becoming an idol. This is where she meets people at the beginning of that journey. She hopes to bring people joy and help Yume through college.
Meet Yume
Yume studies business, plays competitively and experiments in the kitchen. Expect a brisk reply and an opinion about your next move.
Built for the web
Open NariYume in your browser. Signed-in accounts have a shared daily allowance for chat, calls and generated activities. Premium provides a larger allowance: US$9.99 monthly or US$94.99 annually.
The web app is the focus of this launch. Short games, study sessions and character reactions give you room to explore between conversations.
Small details matter
Much of the work happens between the obvious features: a reply arriving sooner, a microphone that starts listening when expected, or a game that fits a smaller screen.
This is an indie project growing through those details. The next steps are guided by how people use it, what feels good and what gets in the way.
