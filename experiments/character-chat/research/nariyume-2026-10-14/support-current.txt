Help & feedback
Questions, bugs and ideas go to the developer at Koro Interactive.
Send a note
In the
web app
, open Settings → Help & feedback → Send feedback or report a bug. Choose a bug, idea or developer note. You can send feedback with a free account; no subscription is required.
The form confirms delivery with a reference number. Your note, selected character and web version are linked to your account in the developer inbox. Chat history, audio and local diagnostics are not attached. Include your account email only if you want a reply.

Common questions
How do I delete my account?
On the web, open
Settings → Data & deletion → Delete account
and confirm your sign-in. In the iOS App, open your profile and use
Delete Account
. Deletion happens right away and is permanent.
      If you can no longer sign in, email
[email protected]
from the address on your account. Web
      conversation history is browser-local; clear it from Settings or remove
      nariyume.com site data in your browser as well.
How do I cancel my subscription?
For an Apple purchase, open
Settings → [your name] →
      Subscriptions
on your iPhone, select NariYume Premium, and tap
      Cancel. For a web purchase, open NariYume Settings and choose
Usage & subscription
, then open billing management for cancellation and payment changes. Deleting
      the App, clearing browser data, or signing out does not cancel a
      subscription.
How do I report a message?
Use the flag button beside a character reply, then choose
Report message
. A record is sent to our moderation queue
      and you’ll see a confirmation. We review reports by hand.
My conversation is stuck or off-the-rails.
Try sending again after checking your connection. If a reply stays stuck,
      report the problem through Settings → Help & feedback before clearing
      anything. Clearing chat and memories is permanent; it is not required to report a bug.
I signed out but my data is still there.
Signing out preserves your account profile and subscription. iOS data
      that is eligible for sync remains in the cloud. Web conversation history
      stays in the browser where it was created and does not follow you to a
      different browser or device.
Voice mode doesn't work on launch.
On the web, opening call mode requests microphone access. Allow it in
      your browser's permission prompt. Transcription is handled online; a
      browser speech-recognition engine or model download is not required.
      If access was blocked, change microphone access in the browser's site
      settings, then try again. Typed chat remains available. For the native
      iOS app, check Settings → NariYume.
I bought Premium but it didn't unlock.
In the web app, choose
Refresh access
after returning
      from Stripe Checkout. For an Apple purchase, use Restore Purchases in
      the iOS App. If access still does not appear, email
[email protected]
from the
      account address and say whether you paid through Apple or Stripe. Do not
      send a full card number.
What AI model powers Nari?
Nari is backed by large-language models accessed through our
      inference proxy. The specific model may change over time as
      we improve quality. See our
Privacy Policy
for how your messages are
      sent to the inference provider and what happens to them.
Outage and status
If the app can't reach our servers, you'll see a banner in the
      chat view. Most outages are transient and resolve within a few
      minutes. If a problem persists, email support and include your device,
      operating-system and browser or app version, plus a description of what
      you were doing. Do not include passwords or payment-card details.
Feedback
We read every piece of feedback. Bug reports with reproduction
      steps, feature ideas, and "this is weird" observations are all
      welcome.
