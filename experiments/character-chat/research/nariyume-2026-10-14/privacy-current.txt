2. Personal data we collect
2.1 Account information
When you sign in using one of the methods we offer (for example Apple,
      Google, X, or email and password), our authentication provider gives us a
      stable account identifier. We store it alongside your chosen display name,
      the birth date you enter during setup (used for the age gate and birthday
      features; only the month/day and a coarse age range are sent to AI
      providers, see §4), and your email address when you or your sign-in
      provider supplies it. We do not receive or store your plaintext password.
      We also use a per-device identifier to manage which sessions are signed in
      to your account.
2.2 Conversation content
Your messages, the AI’s replies, saved memories (facts, entities and
      relationships extracted from conversations), and metadata about your
      character’s mood and affection state are saved so the App can
      continue a conversation across launches and devices. This content may be
      stored on your device, in your browser, and with our cloud storage
      providers under your account identifier. Guest activity remains local.
      Your account profile and subscription entitlement are stored with our
      cloud providers. Section 5 describes how content may be reviewed.
2.3 Microphone and speech data
When you use voice features, audio is captured by your device’s
      microphone and converted to text, either on your device or by our
      speech-to-text service providers. We do not store these recordings in our
      application database. Providers may retain limited data under their own
      terms for reliability and abuse monitoring. The resulting transcript is
      handled like a typed message (see §4). You can use typed input instead.
2.4 Usage and diagnostics
For signed-in accounts, we keep content-free service records of account
      access, generation requests and outcomes, usage limits, checkout creation
      and completion, and payment status. These records use protected account
      references and help us reconcile usage, billing and service failures.
      They do not depend on browser measurement. Retention depends on
      the category and collection date, as described in section 8.
      They contain no conversation text, audio, memories or raw account ID.
      Recording a service event does not enable advertising or grant permission
      to share it with advertising services.
Our apps and website use first-party and service-provider analytics to
      record bounded usage steps, errors and timings so we can keep the service
      working and improve it. Product measurement is part of the service in every
      region and is not an optional setting: by using the App you agree to it
      under our Terms of Service. If you do not want it, please stop using the App
      and request deletion. It excludes conversation text, voice recordings,
      memories, names and email addresses. Random browser identifiers and
      protected account references link permitted steps across sign-in and
      checkout. Section 8 describes event storage and deletion. Our
      infrastructure providers may create ordinary security, performance and
      request logs. We do not fingerprint your device.
Advertising and social media partners.
A separate
      advertising setting lets us share limited data with the advertising and
      social media platforms we use to promote NariYume (“advertising
      partners”). When advertising measurement is enabled, we may share:
      events (visits, sign-ups, engagement milestones such as reaching the daily
      free limit or opening our plans page, checkout starts, and purchases with
      their value and currency); and identifiers (browser and device information,
      network addresses, advertising click identifiers, protected account
      references, and a one-way hashed version of your email address that
      partners use only to match you with your account on their platform). A hash
      is a matching identifier, not anonymous data. Partners use this data to
      measure our advertising, improve which people see our ads, and build or
      exclude audiences, including audiences of similar people. Some partners’
      website scripts run on our site and may set advertising cookies; they
      receive only the product events described here, not conversations or form
      fields. We never share conversations, voice recordings, memories, or your
      plain-text email address with advertising partners, and we do not build
      audiences from your conversations. Advertising click identifiers are kept
      in your browser for up to 30 days and are encrypted on our servers for the
      same period. We receive campaign reports from our advertising partners. A
      current list of our advertising partners is available on request (see §15). Our
Data Usage Policy
summarises this sharing.
Advertising measurement is enabled by default, including before you sign
      in, subject to opt-out and Global Privacy Control, except in the European
      Economic Area, the United Kingdom (including Gibraltar and the Crown
      Dependencies), Switzerland and Quebec. There, it is enabled only after you
      choose to allow advertising, and we will ask again before relying on that
      choice for a new kind of advertising use. Advertising measurement uses browser identifiers and is not anonymous.
      Refusing it does not restrict app access. Use the Your Privacy Choices
      button on this page, accessible from Settings or the site footer, and select
      Essential only to opt out of all optional advertising. Opting out or
      withdrawing consent stops our future advertising data sharing, removes
      advertising scripts and clears known advertising cookies; audience lists
      are updated on the next refresh. Financial records may be retained
      separately where needed for billing and legal obligations.
2.5 Purchase information
Purchases are handled by app stores (for in-app purchases) and by our
      payment processors (on the web). They share customer, subscription,
      payment-status and transaction identifiers with us so we can grant access
      and manage billing. We do not receive your full payment-card number.
2.6 Optional device data
The App can read from several optional sources on your device to
      make your character feel more aware of your day. Each of these is
off by default
, requires the relevant iOS
      permission, and is used only for the feature described below. You
      can turn any of them on or off at any time in iOS Settings or from
      within the App, and we do not store the underlying records on our
      servers.
Calendar (optional, read-only).
If you grant
        access, the App reads upcoming events so your character can
        anticipate your day (for example, acknowledging a meeting or a
        trip). A short, calendar-derived summary may be included in the
        context sent to our AI inference provider (see §4) so replies can
        reference what you have coming up. We do not upload your full
        calendar.
Approximate Location (optional).
If you grant
        access, the App uses your approximate location to provide local
        context such as the weather. A short, location-derived summary
        (for example, a city name or current conditions) may be included
        in the context sent to our AI inference provider (see §4). We do
        not continuously track or store your precise location.
Photos (optional).
Only a photo that you
        explicitly choose to attach to a message is sent inline with that
        single AI request so the character can respond to it. The App
        does not browse, scan, or upload your photo library, and no other
        photos are accessed.

4. AI providers
AI responses, voice and related features are generated using third-party
      AI model and voice providers, and in some cases our own servers. The App
      does not contact these providers directly: requests pass through
      infrastructure we operate or control, which routes them to the providers
      we use from time to time. On first use the App asks you to agree to AI
      processing before any of it happens, and nothing is sent until you do.
      Because AI processing is what the App is, this is an agreement rather than
      a switch. You can review the notice and acceptance date in the App’s
      privacy settings.
Depending on the feature you use, a request may include: the text of your
      messages and the recent conversation context; a photo you have explicitly
      attached; the first name you chose in the App and any saved name
      pronunciation; your birthday
day
(month and day only) and a coarse
      age range; things your character has learned about you in conversation
      (memories) and notes you have saved; and, only if you have enabled them, a
      short summary of optional device context such as weather or your next
      calendar event. A technical identifier may accompany requests for rate
      limiting, entitlement enforcement, security and cost measurement. We do
not
send your email address, sign-in identifiers, full
      birth date, raw location coordinates or your calendar as a whole to these
      providers.
We configure our AI routing so that providers do not store or train on
      your requests where that option is available. Providers process requests
      to generate replies and may keep limited operational logs under their own
      retention and security policies. Personalized reply text and generated
      audio are not intentionally stored in our application cache; shared
      scripted recordings may be cached for playback.
5. Content review, safety and reports
We and service providers acting on our behalf may review account data,

      reports, bugs and errors, respond to support requests, comply with law, and
      improve the quality of the App and its characters. Access is limited to
      people who need it for these purposes.
Every AI-generated message exposes a Report action. When you report a

      your account identifier (if signed in) and a timestamp for review. When
      you use the feedback form or email support, we keep what you send, related
      account and app details, and your email address if you share it, to
      investigate and reply.

11. Security
We use platform secure storage on your device, managed authentication
      sessions, access rules and server-side authorization for cloud data, and
      encryption in transit (TLS) for network requests. To prevent abuse of free
      allowances (fraud prevention), the web app stores a random device
      identifier in your browser and sends it with requests to our servers; it
      is kept for up to 30 days after its last use and is not used for
      advertising. No system is perfectly secure; we
      encourage you to use a unique, strong password and enable
      two-factor authentication with your sign-in provider where available.
If we become aware of a personal-data breach that is likely to
      result in significant harm to you, we will notify you in
      accordance with applicable law (including the PDPA’s data
      breach notification obligation).
